    Our Museum

    Sever do Vouga's Municipal Museum is a dynamic cultural center, compromised to tell the History of a territory, disclosing its heritage and elements of culturaly and ethnographic interest, but also a place of investigation, which protects and makes known the heritage of Sever do Vouga.

    It addresses archaeology in Terras de Sever, the industrial and mining heritage, ethnography and cultural practices, the natural wonders of the territory and its dedication to small fruits (with emphasis on the blueberry); supported by a unique museum collection, and with the use of new technologies.

    The Municipal Museum incorporates in its collections not only archaeological finds, but also pieces related to the economic activities that marked our municipality in the 19th and 20th centuries.

    Among the archaeological sites represented in our collections, we highlight: Rôdo, Anta de Cerqueira, Dolmen 2 do Chão Redondo, Mamoa da Terrainha, Anta da Capela dos Mouros, Vale da Minhata, Castelão (Cedrim), Minas da Malhada, and Necrópole de Cima.

    With regard to economic activities, there are representative pieces of local industries, animal husbandry systems, and forestry exploitation, the pasta industry and wine exploitation.

    The Museum has received some donations that are enriching its collection. If you would like to donate any for documentary use, please contact us.
